# Reelscore SEO audit — 20 November 2026

Site: https://reelscore-xi.vercel.app · Method: claude-seo v2.6.2 audit process. Tools run: `render_page.py`,
`page_checks.py`, `sitemap_discovery.py`, `agentic_check.py`, `preload_check.py`, `validate-schema.py`, plus
`scripts/seo-audit.mjs` across all 3,839 pre-rendered pages. Business type: publisher / review platform.

Scope note: the audit ran against an exact build of the site from the live catalogue (2,668 titles, 1,091 people)
served with the same routing and headers as Vercel, because the audit machine could not reach the live domain.
Core Web Vitals, live response headers, TLS and Search Console data were therefore not measured; see
`seo/README.md` for running those against the live URL.

## What works

- Every title and every person with 2+ titles has a pre-rendered page with its own title, description,
  self-referencing canonical, Open Graph tags and valid JSON-LD (claude-seo's schema validator passes them).
- Sitemap: 3,842 URLs, valid, matches the pages, declared in robots.txt.
- robots.txt keeps admin, My List and data files out; all search and AI crawlers can reach content.
- Real 404 status for unknown paths outside the app's routes; clean URLs; no redirect chains.
- Ratings only enter search markup at 10+ verdicts, as decided.
- Title pages link to people, people to titles, and each title to 12 similar titles.

## Findings

### High

1. **The home, Browse and People pages have no content without JavaScript** (Technical, AI). Their HTML is the app
   shell: 46 words and links only to the menu. Of 3,839 pages, none can be reached from the home page through plain
   links; crawlers find them only through the sitemap. Google renders JavaScript, so it does see more, but late and
   less reliably, and AI fetchers that don't run scripts see nothing.
   *Fix:* pre-render these three like title pages: the home page with its rows (in theatres, coming soon, popular),
   each Browse section with links to every title in it, and People with links to every person page.
2. **Thin pages: median 63 words per title page, 28 per person page** (Content). 2,554 of 2,668 title pages have no
   synopsis; only 1 person has a bio. Google has no word-count rule, but these pages say little beyond facts found on
   many other sites.
   *Fix:* write short original synopses, starting with the most visited and newest titles; add bios for the Popular
   list; show verdicts and reviews once they exist (that is the content only Reelscore has).
3. **The browser title differs from the page title** (Technical, from `render_page.py`'s JavaScript check). The
   pre-rendered page says “Jailer (2023) – Verdicts & Reviews | Reelscore”, then the app replaces it with
   “Jailer — Reelscore”. Google indexes the rendered version, so the year and keywords are lost.
   *Fix:* make the app set the same title format as the build (title, person and list pages).

### Medium

4. **Missing titles return a normal page** (soft 404). `/movie/<anything>` answers 200 with the app shell, which
   then shows nothing. *Fix:* when the app finds no title or person, add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`
   and show a proper “not found” message.
5. **No preview image on 3,643 of 3,839 pages** (On-page). Only 80 titles have a poster and 116 people a photo, so
   shared links show no image. *Fix:* a site-wide default share image now; later, generated poster cards (the
   typographic poster from the redesign) per title.
6. **12 pages share a title with another page**: same name and year in two languages (for example
   Guru 1980 Hindi and Telugu, Vikram 1986 Tamil and Telugu). *Fix:* add the language when a name and year repeat.
7. **No About or “How scores work” page** (Content, trust). *Fix:* one page covering who runs Reelscore, how
   verdicts become a score (including the 10-verdict threshold) and how reviews are moderated; link it from the footer.
8. **Only 196 of 3,839 pages have any image** (Images); see 5.
9. **Every visitor loads the admin code** (Performance, from the page source). `admin.js` (23 KB compressed) and the
   full catalogue (190 KB compressed) load on every page. The catalogue is needed for the app; admin code is not.
   *Fix:* load `admin.js` only on `/admin`.
10. **Layout shift when the app takes over** (Performance, likely). The app replaces the pre-rendered content with
    its own layout once data arrives, which can count against CLS. *Fix:* measure with the live audit first; if it
    shows, keep the pre-rendered block until the app's version is ready and match their top sections.

### Low

11. **518 titles are over 60 characters** and may be cut off in results. *Fix:* drop “– Verdicts & Reviews”
    when the title is long.
12. **Two H1s on every page**: the hidden admin heading in the page shell plus the content's. *Fix:* make the admin
    heading a div.
13. **Admin links in every page's HTML.** They are blocked by robots.txt; render them only for admins.
14. **No WebSite schema on the home page.** Add WebSite (with the site name) and Organization.
15. **No `/llms.txt`.** Optional community file; generate it at build with a summary and key links.
16. **Poster images lack `fetchpriority="high"`** (from `preload_check.py`). Small speed gain on the first screen.
17. **No named robots.txt groups for AI crawlers** (from `agentic_check.py`). All AI crawlers, including those that
    train models, are allowed. That is a policy choice: if you want AI search visibility but no training use, block
    `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `CCBot` and `Google-Extended` and keep `OAI-SearchBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`,
    `PerplexityBot` allowed.
18. **Security headers**: `X-Content-Type-Options` and `Referrer-Policy` are set; there is no `X-Frame-Options` /
    CSP `frame-ancestors`. HSTS and TLS were not checked (live only). Security headers are not a ranking signal.
