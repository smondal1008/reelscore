>
> - Item 5 detail: correction to the diagnosis below. The 82 links were not slug mismatches. They pointed at the 82 thin titles that are `noindex` and left out of the prerender. Those titles are now shown as plain text (no link) in "More like this", person filmographies and lists, and are skipped as similar-title suggestions.

1. **Home page images are full-size originals.** Images on the home page add up to about 50 MB decoded. Single files reach 4.5 MB (Cloudinary `/upload/` URLs with no transformation). This alone explains the 14.6 s LCP.
   *Fix:* insert `f_auto,q_auto,w_1280` (banners) or `w_400` (posters) after `/upload/` when rendering, and lazy-load rows below the fold. This is about 30 lines in `app/main.js` and `build.mjs`.
2. **Thin pages.** 8,975 of 9,185 pages have under 150 words of content. Title pages run about 80–130 words.
   *Fix:* the content only Reelscore has is verdicts and reviews, so getting the first verdicts in matters most. Also add where-to-watch and cast roles, and write longer synopses for the 200 most-visited titles.
3. **Every page downloads the full 3.6 MB catalogue** (brotli-compressed in transit).
   *Fix:* split it into a small home file (current rows) plus per-section files loaded on demand.

## Medium

4. **VideoObject data.**
   - `uploadDate` is the date the trailer was added to Reelscore, not the date YouTube published it.
   - The video is only inserted when someone clicks, so Google may report "no video detected".
   *Fix:* store the YouTube publish date, and render a lightweight YouTube embed in the page HTML on title pages.
5. **82 internal links have no page behind them.** Examples: `/movie/zamaanat-and-justice-for-all-2025` and `/movie/bhoga-khirikee-2019`. They are the old catalogue titles whose stored data is wrong, and their slugs no longer match their links.
   *Fix:* correct or delete those titles in the admin.
6. **Posters.** 610 of the 1,123 titles from 2020 onwards still have no poster. No free source allows commercial reuse. Options are distributor press kits (which the admin already supports, with a permission note) or a paid TMDB commercial licence.
7. **Duplicate titles.** Some titles appear twice with different years, for example Jhund 2020/2022, Attack: Part 1 2020/2022 and Drishyam 2 2021 (Malayalam) vs 2022 (Hindi). The first two pairs should be merged. The Malayalam Drishyam 2 needs its own trailer.
8. **Security headers.** There is no Content-Security-Policy, `frame-ancestors`/X-Frame-Options or COOP header (Lighthouse Best Practices).

## Low

9. **Accessibility.** The home page has no `<main>` landmark, and one link has non-descriptive text.
10. **Missing trailers.** 196 titles from 2020 onwards still have no trailer, mostly daily soaps, reality shows and small regional releases. Those can be added in the admin.
11. **Search Console.** No search data yet. Move to your own domain, verify it in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and submit the sitemap.
